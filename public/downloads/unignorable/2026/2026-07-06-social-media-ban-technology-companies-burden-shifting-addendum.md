## Catalogue note

This addendum is attached to the analysis *A child-safety ban that risks hiding children rather than making platforms safe*.

It examines the claim that government action is taking power from technology companies and giving it back to parents. The addendum argues that the legal responsibility partly moves onto platforms, but the practical burden of safety can still move onto users through age checks, access loss, household enforcement, privacy risks, displacement and appeals.

The core distinction is between **safety at source**, where risky platform design changes before children encounter it, and **safety at the gate**, where users must prove age or lose access.
