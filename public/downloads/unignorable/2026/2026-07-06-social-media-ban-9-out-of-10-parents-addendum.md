## Catalogue note

This addendum is attached to the analysis *A child-safety ban that risks hiding children rather than making platforms safe*.

It examines the government’s “9 out of 10 parents” claim and argues that the statistic is doing more political work than the evidence can safely carry. The addendum distinguishes strong parental demand for action from national consensus, child consent, policy effectiveness and evidence that a blanket ban is the best-supported solution.
