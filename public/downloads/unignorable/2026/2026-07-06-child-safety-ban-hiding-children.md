## Catalogue note

This analysis examines proposals for a child-safety social media ban and asks whether a broad access restriction would reduce harm or move children out of visible, regulated spaces while leaving unsafe platform design largely intact.

The core argument is that children need protection from harmful platform design, but they also need safe access to friendship, learning, creativity, identity, help, privacy and graduated agency.
