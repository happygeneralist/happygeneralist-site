## Catalogue note

This analysis responds to the Institute for Government paper *Reducing school absence* and reframes school absence as a needs crisis rather than only a delivery, monitoring or attendance problem.

The core argument is that attendance policy risks failure if it treats absence as defiance before it treats it as evidence of unmet needs around safety, health, belonging, SEND support, poverty, transport, family stress and trust.
