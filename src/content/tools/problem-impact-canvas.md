The Problem Impact Canvas is a structured framing tool that helps individuals, teams and organisations make sense of complex challenges before rushing into solutions.

It helps people clarify what is happening now, who is affected, why the situation matters, what needs to change and what outcomes would show meaningful improvement.

## Use it when

- people are jumping to solutions before agreeing what the problem is
- goals feel vague, aspirational or disconnected from reality
- different stakeholders hold different assumptions about the problem
- a team needs to define outcomes before moving into delivery
- a project needs a clearer problem frame before experimentation begins

## How it works

The canvas helps teams move through a structured set of prompts:

1. Identify affected parties.
2. Describe the current reality.
3. Explain the current impact.
4. Name the main tension.
5. Describe the preferred reality.
6. Identify the key needs.
7. Define the outcomes.
8. Summarise the work in a driver statement.

The result is a shareable artefact that can align stakeholders, guide experimentation or inform strategy.

## What it helps produce

The two most important outputs are:

- a short driver statement that explains the central challenge and why action is needed
- two or three outcomes that describe what meaningful improvement would look like

## Design logic

The canvas slows down solutionism and makes the problem space more explicit.

It does this by separating the current reality from the preferred reality, surfacing the tension between them, identifying who is affected, and translating needs into outcomes.

This tool is part of a wider Happygeneralist method system. The public page intentionally exposes only the user-facing layer needed to understand and start using the canvas.
