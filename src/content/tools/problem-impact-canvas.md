The Problem Impact Canvas is a practical, human-centred tool designed to help individuals, teams and organisations make sense of complex challenges before rushing into solutions.

It provides a structured way to clarify what is happening, who is affected, why it matters and what kinds of outcomes need to be considered.

The canvas is useful when a problem is visible but still poorly framed. It helps slow the jump from concern to solution by creating space to understand impact first.
