This archive collects working applications of the Unignorable framework to public topics, reports, speeches, policies and institutional narratives.

The archive is deliberately lightweight. The site provides catalogue notes and context. The downloadable PDFs hold the full analysis.

## How to read these analyses

These analyses are interpretive, not neutral reporting. Some are LLM-assisted and should be read as structured analysis rather than verified journalism.

The purpose is to test whether hidden needs, displaced burdens, legitimacy gaps and patterns of institutional erasure can be made easier to see.

## 2026

### School absence is framed as a delivery problem, but it is really a needs crisis

An Unignorable analysis of school absence framing, responding to the Institute for Government paper *Reducing school absence*.

The analysis argues that absence should be read as evidence of unmet needs around safety, health, belonging, accessibility, SEND support, poverty, family support and trust, rather than only as a delivery or attendance problem.

- Lenses: needs erasure, burden transfer, false efficiency, leadership theatre, luxury of disconnection

## Archive practice

Each analysis should have two files:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic-slug.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic-slug.md
```

The PDF is the public artefact. The Markdown sidecar holds metadata for archive pages, Labs references and future indexing.
