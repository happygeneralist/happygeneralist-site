## Stub

User needs erasure is a working term for the pattern where some needs are ignored, minimised, reframed, individualised or made illegible inside a policy, project, strategy, speech or service design.

The need may still exist in lived experience, evidence or operational reality, but it is no longer treated as a legitimate input to decision-making.

## Why it matters

Many public-service conflicts are framed as stakeholder disagreement, competing priorities, communication failure or implementation difficulty.

Often, underneath those conflicts, some needs are being erased.

## Link to Civic Design Intelligence

This is one of the theoretical problems Civic Design Intelligence may help expose by keeping needs, evidence and decisions more traceable.
