This archive collects machine-generated working analyses produced using the Unignorable custom LLM.

The archive is deliberately lightweight. The site provides catalogue notes and context. The downloadable PDFs hold the full generated analyses.

## How to read these analyses

These analyses are generated using the Unignorable custom LLM. They are the experiment: a way of testing whether a custom civic-analysis model can make hidden needs, displaced burdens, legitimacy gaps and patterns of institutional erasure easier to see.

They are interpretive outputs, not neutral reporting or verified journalism. Publication in this archive means they have been selected and lightly reviewed as useful working artefacts, not that every claim should be treated as independently settled.

## Archive practice

Each analysis should have two files:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic-slug.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic-slug.md
```

The PDF is the public artefact. The Markdown sidecar holds metadata for archive pages, Labs references and future indexing.
