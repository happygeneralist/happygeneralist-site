## Working definition

Discovery fatigue is the exhaustion, disengagement and loss of trust that develops when people are repeatedly asked to explain a problem, contribute evidence or revisit the same questions without seeing meaningful action or accumulated learning.

It can affect service users, practitioners and delivery teams. The problem is not discovery itself. It is discovery without memory, consequence or a credible route to decision.

## A useful distinction

Discovery fatigue is often treated as reluctance to participate. It may instead be evidence that an organisation keeps resetting what it knows rather than acting on it.
