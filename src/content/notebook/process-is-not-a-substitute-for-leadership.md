## Short entry

Process is not a substitute for leadership.

Research, discovery, consultation, workshops and governance can improve the quality of a decision. They cannot decide who is responsible, resolve contested trade-offs, assign authority or make an institution act on what it already knows.

Process becomes a substitute for leadership when completing the approved activities is treated as the outcome, while the underlying decision remains unowned and the service or outcomes remain unchanged.

A useful test is:

> What decision will this process enable, and who will own it when the process ends?
