## Spark

Process is not a substitute for leadership.

Research, consultation, workshops and governance can improve the quality of a decision. They cannot decide who is responsible, resolve a contested trade-off or make an institution act on what it already knows.

Process becomes a substitute for leadership when completing the approved activities is treated as the outcome. The work can look responsible while the underlying decision remains unowned and the service remains unchanged.

The test is simple:

> What decision will this process enable, and who will own it when the process ends?
