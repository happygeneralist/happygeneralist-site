---
title: Requirements are not strategy
description: A starting idea about the difference between complying with requirements and designing a coherent direction.
date: 2026-07-04
status: emerging
maturity: draft
themes:
  - strategy
  - public service
tags:
  - requirements
  - strategy
idea_type: distinction
core_claim: Requirements can define obligations, but they do not explain who the service is for, what journey it supports or what change it is trying to create.
useful_when:
  - A statutory or organisational requirement is being mistaken for a strategy.
  - A website or service exists but does not have a clear theory of support.
---

A requirement can tell you that something must exist.

It does not tell you what experience it should create, which people it should support, what decisions it should help them make or how success should be judged.
